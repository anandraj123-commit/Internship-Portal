# Applied internship SEO keywords

Approval wording is based on the owner’s confirmation that parent company Apurva Software Solutions is AICTE approved for internships. It does not describe certificates as AICTE-issued or individual programmes as separately approved.

## Search-volume status

All keywords below are applied, relevant long-tail targets. High/medium search-volume classifications are **unverified**: no Google Ads Keyword Planner or other measured keyword-volume dataset was available. Search results establish topic relevance, not search counts. Validate India-targeted monthly volumes before assigning volume bands.

Research references:
- https://internship.aicte-india.org/ (internship topic and domain context)
- https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575 (monthly search metrics depend on date range, location and network)
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide (natural content; no keyword stuffing or meta-keywords tags)
